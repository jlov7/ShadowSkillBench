# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and technically rigorous
community. Participate in good faith, assume care before malice, and keep
feedback focused on ideas, evidence, and behavior rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation,
personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information, and attempts
to bypass the project's safety, privacy, or research-integrity boundaries.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other
proportionate action when this standard is violated. Report concerns privately
to the project maintainers through the hosting platform's private reporting
mechanism; do not include credentials, private traces, or confidential policy
documents in a report.

This code of conduct applies wherever a person represents the project.
